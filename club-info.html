RULES OF THE DOJO

The Code of Conduct for RMUTP Martial Artists

gavel

01. RESPECT THE MAT

Bow upon entering and leaving the dojo. The mat is a sacred space for growth. Cleanliness and order are mandatory before and after every session.

02. PUNCTUALITY

To be late is to disrespect the instructor's time. Arrive 15 minutes early.

03. HYGIENE

Keep your uniform (Gi) pristine. Personal hygiene is a sign of respect for training partners.

04. NO EGO

We leave our pride at the door. We learn from defeat and remain humble in victory. We protect our training partners.
